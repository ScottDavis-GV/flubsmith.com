FlubSmith is a desktop app for anyone who reads a script aloud: narration, voice-over, podcasts, courses. It scrolls your script while you record, then lets you re-record only the lines you fluffed and drops each fix into the take so nobody can hear the join.

What it does

A prompter and an editor in one window

Most people who read to a microphone use one program to see the script and another to clean up the recording. FlubSmith does both, and knows where in the script each second of audio came from.

A proper teleprompter

Smooth scrolling at a speed you set in lines per minute, a reading-line guide, and a prompter window that fills a second screen, mirrored for beam-splitter glass if you use one.

Retakes that drop into place

Select a fluffed phrase and read it again. You hear a few seconds of run-up in your headphones while the script scrolls exactly as it did, and the new reading is joined in at the pauses either side.

Finds the flubs for you

After a take, FlubSmith listens to it and lines it up with your script. It lists the passages you said twice, the stray “um”s and the lines you skipped, and cuts a repeated start with one click.

Finished, ready to publish

Voice finishing evens out loud and quiet phrases and exports at a standard loudness, such as −19 LUFS for a podcast, with peaks held below −1 dB. Your original recording is never changed.

Your phone as a remote

Scan a code and your phone becomes a remote over your own Wi-Fi: play, pause, speed, record and mark a flub, from where you stand at the microphone. Nothing to install.

Stays on your computer

Speech is recognised on your own machine, and your scripts and recordings are files you keep. FlubSmith has no account and no analytics, and never sends your scripts or recordings anywhere.

Fixing a flub

Keep reading. Fix it afterwards.

  1. Record

    The script scrolls and the microphone records together. Read straight through.

  2. Mark

    Fluff a line? Press X and carry on. The mark lands on the waveform.

  3. Retake

    Select the phrase, hear the run-up, read it again, press Space.

  4. Export

    Play it back with the joins, then export a WAV at the loudness you need.

FlubSmith records audio only. Speech features work in English. Takes save as one file with the audio, the script and every mark, so you can come back to them another day.
