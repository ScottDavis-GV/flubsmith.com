Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short: FlubSmith runs on your computer. Your scripts and recordings are kept there, speech is recognised there, and the app sends nothing over the internet. It has no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and neither does this website.

Who we are

FlubSmith is made by TechniQuest Associates ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to hello@techniquestassociates.com.

The microphone

  • FlubSmith uses the microphone only while you are recording a take or a retake. It asks for permission the first time.
  • It records audio only. It never asks for or uses a camera.

Where your work is kept

  • Scripts and takes. Scripts are text files and takes are .tptake files, saved where you choose. We never receive them.
  • Autosave. While you record, the take in progress is saved to the app's own folder on your computer, so it can be recovered after a crash. It is removed once the take is saved or discarded.
  • Settings. Your settings and recent files list are kept in the app's settings folder on your computer.

Speech recognition

To find flubs and to follow your voice, FlubSmith recognises speech with a model that is installed with the app and runs on your computer. Your recordings are not sent to any speech service.

The phone remote

  • The phone remote is off until you switch it on. When it is on, FlubSmith answers on your local network only, and only to a phone that has the pairing code in the QR code it shows.
  • The remote can control playback and recording. It cannot see your script, your recordings or your files.
  • Switching the remote off and on again makes a new pairing code and disconnects every phone.

What FlubSmith never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No connection to our servers or anyone else's. The app does not check for updates by itself.
  • We never receive your scripts or recordings, so we cannot use, sell or share them.

Buying

FlubSmith is in development and is not yet on sale. Before it is, this policy will be updated to say exactly what, if anything, buying or licensing it involves sending.

This website

flubsmith.com has no cookies, analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers, like any web server, see your IP address to send you the page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message and address to reply and to keep a record of our conversation. If you ask to hear when FlubSmith is ready, we use your address for that and nothing else. We do not share your details with anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Everything the app keeps is on your computer and stays until you delete it: your script and take files, and the app's settings folder after uninstalling.
  • To have us delete emails you have sent us, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to its use. Write to us and we will help.

Children

FlubSmith is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here with a new effective date.
