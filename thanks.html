Getting your license key…

This page shows your license key after you buy, renew or upgrade FlubSmith. The link that brought you here has no order in it.

If you have just paid, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your receipt and we'll send your key.

Adding the key to FlubSmith

  1. Open FlubSmith.
  2. In the settings panel on the right, find License.
  3. Paste the key into Add a license key and choose Add key.

A key works on up to three of your computers. To move it to another, choose Remove beside it on the old one first.

Save this key now, for example in a password manager. If you lose it, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your license ID or your receipt and we'll send it again.

Getting your updated key

For your security, a renewed or upgraded key is not shown here. Your copy of FlubSmith collects it, using the key it already has:

  1. Open FlubSmith on a computer where your license is already set up.
  2. In the settings panel on the right, find License.
  3. Choose Check for updated key.

Do this on each of your computers. It doesn't use up another computer. If your license isn't set up anywhere just now, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your license ID and receipt.
